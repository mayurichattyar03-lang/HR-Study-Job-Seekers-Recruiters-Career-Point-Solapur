# 👥 HR Study - Role of HR in Bridging Gap Between
# Job Seekers and Recruiters
## Career Point Placement Services, Solapur

---

## 🎯 Objective
To study the role of HR in bridging the gap between 
job seekers and recruiters at Career Point Placement 
Services, Solapur.

## ❓ Problem Statement
There is often a significant gap between what job seekers 
expect and what recruiters look for. This study examines 
how HR professionals can bridge this gap effectively.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Google Sheets
- Recruitment Portals
- MS Word

## 📊 Study Performed
- ✅ End-to-end recruitment process analysis
- ✅ Candidate sourcing and shortlisting
- ✅ Interview scheduling and coordination
- ✅ Campus drive support
- ✅ Employer branding analysis
- ✅ Candidate engagement study

## 📈 Key Findings
- HR plays a critical role in matching candidate 
  skills with job requirements
- Proper communication between recruiters and 
  job seekers reduces hiring time
- Employer branding significantly impacts 
  candidate attraction

## 💡 What I Learned
- End-to-end recruitment process
- HR tools and candidate database management
- Employer branding strategies
- Practical HR operations and employee management
- Campus recruitment coordination
